QA specimen: not a design example.

Every block of the apple-design web kit in every state, light and dark side by side. It breaks design rules on purpose (dozens of glass surfaces, states forced with [data-pressed] and the specimen-only .force-focus class, overlays shown static). For a real screen, open demo.html; to build a page, run build_kit.py --markup <blocks>.
